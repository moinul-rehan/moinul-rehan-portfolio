[01] How it works 01 / 02

From first call to final handoff.

Five steps, no surprises. You always know what is happening next, who owes what, and when it lands.

  1. 01

    Book a call

    We talk through the project, your goals, and what is actually not working. Thirty minutes, and no pitch at the end of it.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    Scope, timeline and price in writing before anything starts — including what is not in it, which is the part that causes trouble later if nobody says it out loud.

  3. 03

    Design

    We work closely. Design updates, feedback and files stay in one place, so you watch the thinking happen instead of waiting for a reveal.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final designs handed over polished, with the components, tokens and annotated specs a developer can genuinely build from.

  5. 05

    Support

    One month of free post-launch support for tweaks, questions or guidance. No stopwatch on it.

[02] FAQ 02 / 02

The questions people actually ask.

Process, timelines and money — answered up front so the first call can be about the work instead.

How do I get started?

Book a short call so we can talk through the project and check we are a good fit. If it is a yes, we put scope, timeline and price in writing, and I start.

How do we communicate and deliver files?

Figma for the work itself, email for anything that needs a record. Final files are handed over from one place, so nothing ends up scattered across four apps.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope, but most projects land between two and four weeks. You get a clear timeline before any work starts.

What if I need help after the project is done?

You get one month of free support after delivery, for small tweaks or questions. No stopwatch on it.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. I usually work 50/50 — half upfront, half on delivery. Larger projects can be split into a custom plan.

What if I am not sure what I need yet?

That is a normal place to start. Book a call and we will work out the right direction from your goals and your budget.
