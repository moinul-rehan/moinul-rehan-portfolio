[04] About 04 / 06

I work in the gap between the brief and the real user.

I'm a product designer based in Dhaka. For the last two years I've designed client products at Softvence IT and now in-house at Tulip Tech — dashboards, fintech, e-commerce and internal tools, mostly for teams outside Bangladesh.

Most of the job happens before Figma opens: working out what the brief actually means, who the real decision-maker is, and which constraint is going to break the design three weeks from now.
